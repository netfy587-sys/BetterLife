<section id="footwork" class="footwork">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div>
      <p class="kicker-sm">The base pattern</p>
      <h2>Six-corner footwork</h2>
      <ol class="fw-list">
        <li><span class="desc"><span class="name">Front left &amp; right</span>Split step, then lunge to the net corners for a drop or net shot, pushing off the back foot to recover.</span></li>
        <li><span class="desc"><span class="name">Mid left &amp; right</span>Side-step to the mid-court for a drive, keeping the racket up through the shot.</span></li>
        <li><span class="desc"><span class="name">Rear left &amp; right</span>Chassé or scissor-step to the back corners for a clear or smash, then recover to base.</span></li>
      </ol>
    </div>

<section id="injury" class="injury">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div>
      <p class="kicker-sm">Playing for the long term</p>
      <h2>Common strain points</h2>
      <ul class="injury-list">
        <li><div><span class="name">Knees</span><p>Repeated lunging strains the patellar tendon — strengthening the quads and calves reduces load on the joint.</p></div></li>
        <li><div><span class="name">Shoulder</span><p>The overhead smash is repetitive and can inflame the rotator cuff — rest and rotator-strengthening work help most.</p></div></li>
        <li><div><span class="name">Ankles</span><p>Lateral movement makes rolled ankles common — court shoes with lateral support cut the risk noticeably.</p></div></li>
        <li><div><span class="name">Lower back</span><p>Explosive rotation during smashes stresses the lumbar spine — core stability work is the usual preventive fix.</p></div></li>
      </ul>
    </div>
